OathByte sound effects and fight music: credits and licences
=============================================================

Every sound in this folder comes from a free pack released under CC0 1.0
(public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
CC0 allows use in any game, free or paid, with no credit required. We credit
the creators anyway. The files were trimmed, made mono, levelled and saved as
MP3 (tools/sfx/build_sfx.py lists which source file each sound comes from).

Where the files came from: the creators' sites could not be reached from the
build machine, so the packs were taken from public GitHub copies of the
original archives: Kenney's packs from the Godot ports
(github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-*-for-godot, original files and CC0 licence),
rubberduck's and artisticdude's packs from game repositories that keep the
original files. For the two OpenGameArt packs, ten of the files were checked
byte for byte (SHA-256) against hashes of the original archives recorded by
an independent project, and all ten matched.

OathByte music: credits and licences
====================================

Picked by Lee on the Music Picker page, November 2026. Each track was trimmed,
made into a seamless loop (or cut short for the level up and fall pieces),
levelled and saved as MP3 (tools/music/build_loops.py makes the files from the
original tracks). Changes were made to every track.
